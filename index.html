COD Safety & Online Charges

The COD Danger: RTO (Return to Origin)

When a customer refuses COD delivery, you lose both forward and return courier charges (~₹120 total). Never ship 100% COD to strangers. Take a small token advance of ₹50 - ₹100 via UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe) to confirm seriousness!

Payment Gateway Charges (Razorpay / Cashfree)

Standard gateway fee is 2% + 18% GST (Net ~2.36%). On a ₹500 product, the gateway takes only ~₹11.80. Direct Google Pay / PhonePe UPI transfer has 0% fee!

Shipping to Gulf (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Oman)

Huge demand for Attar, Kasavu sarees, and Kerala Spices among Malayali expats. Use India Post International EMS or local Kerala Gulf cargo agents (rates: ₹900 - ₹1,400 per kg). Attars without alcohol pass customs easily.
