Islamic Business Code of Conduct:
  • Zero Riba (No Interest): Conventional bank interest, fixed guaranteed returns, or usury are strictly prohibited.
  • Mudarabah & Musharakah Principles: Profits shared on agreed percentages; losses strictly proportional to capital invested.
  • Truthfulness & Amanah: Any product defects must be disclosed honestly; weights & measures must be strictly accurate.
  • Strict Moderation: Strictly business-related discussions only; no spam, politics, or irrelevant forwards.
